### Why This Founder for This Problem

Most education technology is built by engineers who consult teachers. Skippy is built by a teacher who became an engineer. This distinction matters because:

1. **The pedagogy isn't bolted on — it's the foundation.** The 4,134 lines of curriculum prompt content reflect 15 years of teaching practice, not a consultant's interpretation of "best practices."

2. **The UX respects teacher reality.** Twenty-minute sessions, one artifact per week, "One Win Then Wrap" — these design decisions come from lived experience with teacher time constraints, not user research reports.

3. **The AI integration is sophisticated, not superficial.** Asher didn't wrap a chatbot in a lesson plan. He built a diagnostic assessment system (SOLO taxonomy), an asynchronous classification pipeline, and a multi-phase conversation architecture — because he understands that effective tutoring requires those components.

4. **Track record of adoption.** TimeSaveAI's 100+ weekly users demonstrate that Asher can build products teachers actually use, not just products that demo well.
