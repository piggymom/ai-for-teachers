# Impact Metrics Framework

## Teacher-Level Outcomes (Direct Measurement)

### 1. AI Literacy Gain
**What we measure:** Change in SOLO taxonomy level from Week 1 diagnostic to Week 6 assessment.
**How:** Skippy's async classifier diagnoses teacher level at session start (pre-structural through extended-abstract). Compare Week 1 diagnosis to Week 6 demonstrated level.
**Target:** 80% of teachers advance at least one SOLO level; 50% advance two or more.
**Why it matters:** SOLO progression is the most rigorous indicator of genuine understanding growth, not just exposure.

### 2. Prompt Engineering Skill
**What we measure:** Quality of teacher-authored prompts across weeks.
**How:** Rubric-score extracted artifacts from Weeks 2, 3, 4, and 5 on four dimensions: Context specificity, Constraint clarity, Command precision, Criteria inclusion (the 4C Framework).
**Target:** Average rubric score increases 40%+ from Week 2 to Week 5.
**Why it matters:** Prompt engineering is the most transferable AI skill. If teachers can craft effective prompts, they can use any AI tool.

### 3. AI Confidence
**What we measure:** Self-reported confidence in using AI for teaching tasks.
**How:** Pre/post survey (10-point scale) covering: "I can use AI to save time on routine tasks," "I can evaluate whether AI output is appropriate for my students," "I can explain to colleagues when and why to use AI."
**Target:** Average confidence increase of 3+ points (on 10-point scale).
**Why it matters:** Confidence predicts sustained adoption. Teachers who feel uncertain stop using tools.

### 4. Course Completion
**What we measure:** Percentage of enrolled teachers completing all 7 weeks.
**How:** Progress tracking system (database records).
**Target:** 70%+ completion rate (vs. typical online PD completion of 30-50%).
**Why it matters:** Completion is a prerequisite for all other outcomes. High completion also signals product-market fit.

### 5. Artifact Utility
**What we measure:** Whether teachers actually use their artifacts in practice.
**How:** 30-day post-completion survey: "How many times have you used an artifact from the course in the past month?" + qualitative examples.
**Target:** 60%+ of teachers use at least one artifact within 30 days.
**Why it matters:** Artifacts are the concrete output. If they go unused, the course produced knowledge without behavior change.

### 6. Independent AI Use
**What we measure:** Frequency and quality of AI tool use outside of Skippy.
**How:** 30/60/90-day follow-up surveys: frequency of AI use, which tools, what tasks, self-assessed quality of interactions.
**Target:** 70%+ of teachers report using AI tools independently at least weekly 90 days post-completion.
**Why it matters:** This is the ultimate success metric — the course builds independence, not dependency.

## Student-Level Outcomes (Downstream Measurement)

### 7. Student Exposure to AI-Enhanced Instruction
**What we measure:** Whether students in treated teachers' classrooms experience AI-informed teaching.
**How:** Student survey (administered by teacher) 3-6 months post-completion: "Does your teacher use AI to help prepare lessons?" "Has your teacher discussed how AI works?"
**Target:** 50%+ of students report awareness of teacher's AI use in instruction.
**Why it matters:** Teacher AI literacy only matters if it reaches students.

### 8. Student Critical AI Thinking
**What we measure:** Students' ability to critically evaluate AI output.
**How:** Brief assessment (3-5 items): show students AI-generated content with errors/biases and assess whether they can identify issues. Compare treated vs. control classrooms.
**Target:** Treated classrooms score 20%+ higher on critical evaluation items.
**Why it matters:** The long-term goal isn't just teacher AI use — it's students who can think critically about AI.

## System-Level Outcomes (Scale Measurement)

### 9. Teacher Recommendation (NPS)
**What we measure:** Net Promoter Score — willingness to recommend to colleagues.
**How:** Standard NPS question at course completion and 90 days post.
**Target:** NPS > 50 (excellent for education products).
**Why it matters:** Word-of-mouth is the primary distribution channel for teacher PD. High NPS signals organic growth potential.

### 10. District Adoption
**What we measure:** Number of school districts adopting AI for Teachers for PD credit.
**How:** Partnership agreements, enrollment data.
**Target:** 3-5 district partnerships within 12 months of pilot completion.
**Why it matters:** District adoption is the path to sustainable scale and revenue.

### 11. Cost Per Teacher
**What we measure:** Total program cost divided by number of teachers served.
**How:** Track all costs (API, hosting, personnel, support) and divide by completers.
**Target:** < $25 per teacher at pilot scale; < $15 per teacher at 1,000+ scale.
**Why it matters:** Cost efficiency is the argument for AI-guided PD over traditional workshops ($500-2,000 per teacher).
