## Budget Narrative

### Why this budget is efficient:

**1. Low technology costs.** AI API costs are approximately $8-15 per teacher for the full 7-week program. This means 100 teachers cost $800-1,500 in AI compute — orders of magnitude less than human-facilitated PD ($500-2,000 per teacher for comparable content).

**2. No content creation costs.** The 7-week curriculum (4,134 lines of prompt content) is already built. Pilot funding goes to validation and refinement, not creation.

**3. Personnel is the primary investment.** This is appropriate for a pilot stage — the founder's time is the critical input for rapid iteration based on teacher feedback.

**4. Research costs are real but necessary.** Rigorous outcome data is the single most important output of the pilot. Every future grant application depends on evidence generated here.

### Scalability note:

At 1,000 teachers, the per-teacher cost drops to approximately $25 (including pro-rated personnel). At 10,000 teachers, it drops below $15. The cost structure is fundamentally different from traditional PD, where scaling requires proportionally more facilitators.

## Budget Variations by Grant Size

### Small Grant ($50K — e.g., Spencer Foundation)
- Founder time: $30K (0.25 FTE, 6 months)
- Technology: $3K
- Research: $8K
- Operations: $5K
- Dissemination: $2K
- Fiscal sponsor: $2K
- **Scope:** 25-teacher micro-pilot with pre/post assessment

### Medium Grant ($150K-250K — e.g., NYCT, NewSchools)
- Full pilot budget as described above
- **Scope:** 50-100 teacher pilot with comprehensive evaluation

### Large Grant ($500K+ — e.g., NSF, EIR)
- Add: full-time research coordinator ($70K)
- Add: external evaluation team ($50K)
- Add: comparison group study design ($30K)
- Add: Year 2 scale-up to 500 teachers ($80K)
- **Scope:** Rigorous RCT or quasi-experimental study with publication-quality results
