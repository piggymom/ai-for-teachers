## Causal Chain

### If → Then Logic

1. **If** we provide teachers with an AI tutor personalized to their teaching context, **then** they will engage more deeply than with generic PD because the content is immediately relevant to their practice.

2. **If** we structure each session around building one concrete artifact, **then** teachers will leave with usable outputs (not just knowledge) because the artifact provides both a learning vehicle and a practical tool.

3. **If** we use diagnostic assessment (SOLO taxonomy) to adapt scaffolding in real time, **then** teachers at all starting levels will learn effectively because they're neither bored nor overwhelmed.

4. **If** we require teachers to test their skills in external AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini), **then** they will develop transferable skills rather than platform-specific habits because the practice environment differs from the learning environment.

5. **If** we guide teachers through a progression from mental models → prompting → planning → feedback → differentiation → ethics, **then** they will develop integrated AI literacy (not isolated skills) because each week explicitly builds on prior weeks.

6. **If** teachers develop integrated AI literacy with transferable skills, **then** they will independently integrate AI into their classroom practice because they have both the capability and the confidence.

7. **If** teachers model thoughtful, critical AI use in their classrooms, **then** students will develop healthier relationships with AI technology because teacher behavior is the strongest predictor of student technology attitudes.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Evidence | Risk if Wrong |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Teachers have 20 min/week for PD | Time-use surveys show teachers have fragmented free time; 20 min fits in a prep period | Low — 20 min is minimal ask |
| AI tutoring can replace human facilitation for skill-building | Research on intelligent tutoring systems shows comparable outcomes for procedural and conceptual skills | Medium — some teachers may need human support |
| SOLO taxonomy effectively diagnoses AI literacy levels | SOLO is validated across domains but not specifically for AI literacy | Medium — may need calibration |
| Teachers will complete 7 weeks | PD completion rates vary widely (30-90%) depending on relevance and time | High — completion is critical; must monitor and intervene early |
| Skills transfer from Skippy to other AI tools | External testing loop is designed to ensure transfer, but not yet validated | Medium — core to value proposition, must validate in pilot |
| School/district support enables participation | Without admin buy-in, teachers may not prioritize | Medium — district partnerships mitigate this |

## Evidence Base

- **Intelligent tutoring systems**: Meta-analysis (VanLehn, 2011) shows ITS produce learning gains comparable to human tutoring (d = 0.76) for well-structured domains
- **Personalized PD**: Research (Darling-Hammond et al., 2017) identifies personalization, active learning, and sustained duration as key factors in effective PD
- **SOLO taxonomy**: Validated across 40+ years of research (Biggs & Collis, 1982) for assessing understanding depth
- **Artifact-based learning**: Constructionist tradition (Papert, 1980) demonstrates that building concrete objects produces deeper understanding
- **Spaced practice**: 7 weeks of ~20 min sessions leverages spacing effect (Cepeda et al., 2006) versus massed workshop delivery
