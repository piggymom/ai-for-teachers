**The Problem**

[Customize opening for funder priorities. Example for education funders:]

Artificial intelligence is transforming every sector of the economy, but the 3.7 million teachers responsible for preparing the next generation have received almost no meaningful training on how to understand, use, or teach about AI. Current professional development — generic workshops and one-off webinars — fails because it's not personalized to teachers' classrooms, produces awareness without capability, and teaches tool-specific tricks rather than transferable skills. Teachers in under-resourced schools are least likely to receive any training at all, widening the AI literacy gap along existing lines of educational inequity.

**Our Solution**

AI for Teachers is a 7-week AI-guided professional development course that teaches K-12 educators practical AI literacy through personalized conversations with an AI tutor named Skippy. Each week, teachers spend approximately 20 minutes in guided dialogue — adapted to their grade level, subjects, experience, and goals — building one concrete, classroom-ready artifact: a prompt template, a lesson planning workflow, a feedback rubric, or a personal AI policy.

The course is grounded in learning science. Skippy uses SOLO taxonomy to diagnose each teacher's understanding level and adapts scaffolding in real time. The curriculum follows a deliberate progression from mental model building to ethical integration, with each week explicitly building on prior learning. Critically, the course teaches independence — teachers test their skills in ChatGPT, Gemini, and other tools, developing transferable capabilities rather than platform-specific habits.

The platform is fully built and functional, with a sophisticated AI architecture including personalized prompt composition, asynchronous conversation classification, and artifact extraction.

**Why Now**

AI tools are already in classrooms — 40% of teachers report using them, but fewer than 25% have received any formal training. Every month without intervention compounds the gap between thoughtful AI integration and unguided experimentation. At approximately $8-15 per teacher in delivery costs, personalized AI literacy training is now economically feasible at scale for the first time.
