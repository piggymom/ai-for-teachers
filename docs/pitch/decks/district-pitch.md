## Slide 2: The Problem You're Solving

Your teachers are in one of three buckets:

1. **Avoidant** — "I don't use AI and I don't want to." (Need: safety, low stakes, small wins)
2. **Dabbling** — "I've tried ChatGPT but I don't know if I'm doing it right." (Need: structure, skill building)
3. **Dependent** — "I use MagicSchool for everything." (Need: transferable skills, not tool-specific tricks)

**Current PD options fail all three:**
- Workshops are too generic (one size fits none)
- "Prompt lists" teach tricks, not skills
- AI tools that generate content for teachers build dependency, not capability
