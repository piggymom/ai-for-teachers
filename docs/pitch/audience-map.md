## Deep Dive: Education Foundations

### Google.org / Schmidt Futures / Gates Foundation

**Hook:** "We built the Duolingo of teacher AI literacy — except instead of gamification, we use learning science."

**Key data points to lead with:**
- 7-week structured curriculum with measurable progression
- ~$5 per teacher for the full course (vs. $50-200 traditional PD)
- SOLO taxonomy classifier provides granular learning data
- Explicitly teaches tool-agnostic skills (external testing in ChatGPT/Gemini)
- Founder is a 15-year classroom teacher, not a tech tourist

**What they'll want to see:**
- Pilot design and data collection plan
- Equity analysis (how it serves under-resourced schools)
- Theory of change diagram
- Cost per teacher at scale projections
- Plan for open-sourcing curriculum (if applicable)

**Objections:**
- "There's no efficacy data yet." → "That's exactly what we're raising for. The pilot is designed to generate IRB-quality data on SOLO level progression, artifact quality, and transfer to independent AI use."
- "How is this different from ISTE's AI courses?" → "ISTE teaches about AI. We teach teachers to use AI by having them build things with an AI tutor that adapts to their level in real time. It's the difference between reading about swimming and getting in the pool with a coach."

## Deep Dive: School Districts

### NYC DOE / Large Urban Districts

**Hook:** "Your teachers are already using ChatGPT. The question is whether they're using it well. We make sure they are — for $5 per teacher."

**Key data points to lead with:**
- Aligns with DOL AI Literacy Framework (February 2026)
- Self-paced, asynchronous — no coverage needed, no scheduling conflicts
- 20 minutes per week — fits into existing PD time
- Every session produces a classroom-ready artifact
- Google SSO — works with existing accounts

**What they'll want to see:**
- Admin dashboard mockup (completion rates, engagement metrics)
- PD credit pathway (how teachers document hours)
- Data privacy documentation (FERPA, COPPA compliance)
- Integration with existing PD tracking systems
- Proof of teacher satisfaction (pilot testimonials)

**Objections:**
- "We already have AI PD." → "Most district AI PD is one-shot workshops. Teachers attend, nod, and forget. Skippy is 7 weeks of personalized coaching that produces a portfolio of artifacts they actually use."
- "What about teachers who are resistant to AI?" → "Skippy meets them where they are. The SOLO taxonomy classifier detects pre-structural understanding and adjusts scaffolding — concrete analogies, step-by-step walkthroughs, celebrating small wins. No teacher is left behind."
- "We need this in Spanish." → "Multilingual support is on the roadmap. The architecture supports it — system prompts can be translated while maintaining pedagogical structure."

## Deep Dive: Tech / Impact Investors

### EdTech VCs / Impact Funds

**Hook:** "Most EdTech AI tools create dependency. We're the capacity play — and we have the architecture to prove it."

**Key data points to lead with:**
- Real-time SOLO taxonomy classifier (zero-latency async architecture)
- 4,134 lines of pedagogical prompt content — this is a curriculum, not a wrapper
- External testing loop as defensible moat (teaches independence)
- $5 unit economics at current scale
- Working product, not a prototype

**What they'll want to see:**
- Technical architecture deep-dive
- User growth projections (B2C → B2B flywheel)
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Revenue model (B2B licensing math)
- Team expansion plan

**Objections:**
- "Claude/GPT will just add this." → "They can't. A general-purpose AI adding a PD course would be like Google Docs adding a creative writing MFA. The pedagogy is the product — 4,134 lines of scaffolded content tied to a diagnostic classification system. That's curriculum IP, not a feature."
- "The TAM is small." → "3.7 million K-12 teachers in the U.S. At $50/teacher/year (B2B), that's a $185M domestic TAM. Add advanced modules, admin tools, and international markets — this is a platform."
- "No lock-in = no retention." → "The external testing loop is why districts trust us. Retention comes from new curriculum (advanced modules), cohort management tools, and the admin analytics dashboard — not from trapping teachers."

## Deep Dive: Government / Federal Grants

### NSF / IES / NYSED / DOL Workforce Innovation

**Hook:** "The DOL just defined AI literacy for the American workforce. We built the tool to deliver it to 3.7 million teachers."

**Key data points to lead with:**
- Direct alignment with DOL AI Literacy Framework (TEN 07-25, February 2026)
- Measurable outcomes at every level (SOLO taxonomy, 4C completion, artifact quality)
- Serves all subjects, grade levels, and experience levels
- Self-paced = equitable access (no travel, no time away from classroom)
- Built on established learning science (SOLO taxonomy: Biggs & Collis, 1982)

**What they'll want to see:**
- Logic model / theory of change
- Research design for efficacy study
- IRB pathway
- Alignment matrix (DOL framework → Skippy curriculum mapping)
- Budget with indirect cost rates
- Letters of support from partner districts

**Objections:**
- "What's the evidence base?" → "SOLO taxonomy has 40+ years of validation in education research. The innovation is applying it to AI literacy through a real-time diagnostic classifier. Our pilot will generate the first empirical data on scaffolded AI literacy PD."
- "Is this just another chatbot?" → "No. This is a structured 7-week curriculum with adaptive scaffolding, artifact-based assessment, and a diagnostic classification system. The AI is the delivery mechanism, not the product."
