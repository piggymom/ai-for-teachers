# Narrative Foundation — AI for Teachers

## The Problem

Teachers are drowning in AI anxiety, not AI tools. 82% of K-12 teachers report feeling unprepared to use AI effectively, yet traditional professional development — one-shot workshops, slide decks, and generic prompt lists — teaches compliance, not capability. The result: teachers either avoid AI entirely or become dependent on specific tools without understanding the transferable skills underneath.

## The Solution

**Skippy** is a personalized AI tutor that teaches teachers to use AI — not by lecturing, but by building. Over a 7-week guided course, each teacher works one-on-one with Skippy to create concrete classroom artifacts (prompt templates, lesson planning workflows, feedback rubrics, differentiation strategies, and a personal AI policy). The pedagogy adapts in real time: a SOLO taxonomy classifier diagnoses each teacher's understanding level and adjusts scaffolding automatically. Every skill is transferable — teachers practice in ChatGPT and Gemini, not just in Skippy.

## Why Now

Three forces are converging:

1. **Federal mandate.** The U.S. Department of Labor released its first-ever AI Literacy Framework in February 2026. States and districts are now scrambling for compliant PD programs.
2. **Teacher retention crisis.** Districts spend $50-200 per teacher per PD session on programs that teachers overwhelmingly describe as "useless." AI-specific PD barely exists.
3. **The dependency trap.** The first wave of AI-in-education tools (Khanmigo, MagicSchool, etc.) generate content for teachers rather than building teacher capacity. When the tool is removed, so is the capability. This is the Ozempic problem — and funders are starting to notice.

## The Vision

Phase 1 (now): Launch the 7-week AI literacy course for individual teachers. Build the user base. Collect learning progression data.

Phase 2 (6 months): Add district admin dashboards, cohort management, PD credit certification, and advanced topic modules (special ed, ELL, STEM labs). Begin B2B sales.

Phase 3 (18 months): Become the standard for teacher AI professional development nationwide. Expand to multilingual markets. Open the curriculum engine for districts to create custom modules. Build the dataset that proves which scaffolding approaches actually move teacher practice.

The long game: **AI for Teachers becomes the place where the profession develops its relationship with AI** — not a chatbot, but a curriculum that scales personalized coaching to every teacher in the country.
