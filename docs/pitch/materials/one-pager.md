## FRONT

### The Problem
Teachers are drowning in AI anxiety, not AI tools. Traditional PD costs $50-200 per session and doesn't work. First-generation AI tools generate content *for* teachers — building dependency, not capability. The DOL's new AI Literacy Framework (Feb 2026) means districts must now address this gap.

### The Solution: Skippy
An AI tutor that teaches teachers to use AI — by building, not watching.

**7 weeks. 20 minutes per session. One classroom artifact per week.**

## BACK

### How It Works

**For Teachers:**
1. Sign in with Google (30 seconds)
2. Complete a quick profile — subject, grade, goals, concerns (5 minutes)
3. Each week, have a 20-minute conversation with Skippy
4. Build one concrete artifact per session
5. Test your skills in ChatGPT/Gemini (the external testing loop)
6. Finish with a portfolio of 7 classroom-ready tools + a personal AI policy

**Under the Hood:**
- SOLO taxonomy classifier diagnoses understanding in real time
- 6-layer prompt engine personalizes every response
- Async conversation ledger tracks phase, level, and skill progression
- Artifact extraction system saves and organizes teacher-created outputs
- 4,134 lines of pedagogical prompt content across 7 week-specific modules
