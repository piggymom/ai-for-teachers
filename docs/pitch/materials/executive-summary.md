# AI for Teachers — Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

The U.S. Department of Labor released its first AI Literacy Framework in February 2026, signaling that AI competency is now a workforce expectation — including for the 3.7 million K-12 teachers in the United States. Districts are scrambling to respond, but the professional development landscape is unprepared: traditional PD costs $50-200 per teacher per session, is delivered in one-shot workshops that teachers describe as ineffective, and AI-specific PD is less than 1% of the $18 billion annual market.

Meanwhile, the first wave of AI-in-education tools has revealed a critical flaw: products like MagicSchool and Khanmigo generate content *for* educators rather than building educator capability. When access is removed, the capability goes with it. This is the dependency trap — and it's the default trajectory for AI in education.

## The Solution

**AI for Teachers** has built **Skippy**, a personalized AI tutor that teaches teachers to use AI effectively through a structured 7-week course. Each 20-minute session is a one-on-one conversation that produces a concrete classroom artifact: prompt templates, lesson planning workflows, feedback rubrics, differentiation strategies, and a personal AI policy.

What makes Skippy different is the learning science underneath:

- **SOLO Taxonomy Classifier:** A real-time diagnostic system (running asynchronously after every teacher response) that assesses the teacher's understanding level across five validated stages and adjusts instruction automatically.
- **6-Layer Personalization Engine:** Every response is shaped by the teacher's subject, grade level, goals, constraints, current understanding level, and conversation phase.
- **External Testing Loop:** Skippy deliberately sends teachers to practice their skills in ChatGPT and Gemini — building tool-agnostic capability that survives platform changes.
- **Artifact-First Design:** Every session produces something the teacher uses in their classroom. Not theory, not slides — a tool they made.

The curriculum comprises 4,134 lines of pedagogical prompt content across 7 week-specific modules, supported by a conversation ledger that tracks learning progression, skill acquisition, and artifact completion.

## Traction and Proof Points

- **TimeSaveAI:** An observation feedback tool built with the same design philosophy by the same founder — currently serving 100+ weekly active users. This validates the approach of AI-augmented professional practice.
- **New Visions PD:** The founder delivered Critical AI Literacy professional development to all staff at New Visions for Public Schools in February 2026, one of New York City's largest education support organizations.
- **Working Product:** Skippy is a fully functional application — not a prototype or mockup. It features Google SSO authentication, personalized onboarding, a 7-week adaptive course, artifact extraction and gallery, podcast recaps, and progress tracking. It is deployed on Vercel with a Supabase backend.
- **Cost Advantage:** The full 7-week course costs approximately $5 per teacher in API costs — a 10-40x improvement over traditional professional development.

## Business Model

**Phase 1 (Current):** Free access for individual teachers to build the user base, generate testimonials, and collect learning progression data.

**Phase 2 (Post-Pilot):** B2B district licensing at $25-50 per teacher per year. The district license includes an admin analytics dashboard (completion rates, engagement metrics, SOLO level distributions), cohort management tools, PD credit documentation, and completion reporting.

**Phase 3 (Scale):** Advanced curriculum modules (special education, ELL, STEM-specific, subject-specific) priced at $10-15 per module. Certification programs. Curriculum licensing platform for districts to create custom modules. Multilingual expansion.

**Unit Economics:** ~$5 in API costs per teacher against $25-50 in B2B revenue yields 80-90% gross margins at scale. The expensive work (curriculum development, classifier architecture, prompt engineering) is already complete. Marginal cost per additional teacher is API calls only.
