# Master Deck Structure — AI for Teachers

## The Master Deck (16 slides)

This is the comprehensive version. All variant decks pull from this structure.

---

### Slide 1: Hook / Opening
**Title:** "What happens when the AI goes away?"
**Content:** One stat, one question. The Ozempic frame.
- "Students using AI tools showed a 17% performance drop when access was removed." (Frame as the core tension)
- "Most AI tools for teachers create the same dependency. We built something different."
**Visual:** Single bold stat on dark background. No logo yet.
**Speaker notes:** This is the "sit up" moment. Don't explain yet — let the question land.

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
**Title:** "Teachers are drowning in AI anxiety, not AI tools"
**Content:**
- 82% of teachers feel unprepared to use AI effectively
- Traditional PD costs $50-200 per teacher per session — and teachers describe it as "useless"
- The DOL just released its first-ever AI Literacy Framework (Feb 2026) — districts have no way to comply
**Visual:** Three stats stacked vertically. Clean, high contrast.
**Speaker notes:** Establish the triple bind: teachers are anxious, PD is broken, and now there's a federal mandate.

---

### Slide 3: The Ozempic Insight
**Title:** "Capacity vs. dependency"
**Content:**
- Most AI-in-education tools generate content FOR teachers
- When the tool is removed, the capability goes with it
- "We don't replace teacher judgment — we develop it"
- The Ozempic problem: suppress the struggle, lose the growth
**Visual:** Two-column comparison. Left: "Dependency model" (tool does the work). Right: "Capacity model" (teacher learns the skill).
**Speaker notes:** This is the intellectual core of the pitch. Take your time here. Name specific competitors if comfortable ("MagicSchool generates lesson plans for you — what happens when your school stops paying?").

---

### Slide 4: The Solution
**Title:** "Meet Skippy"
**Content:**
- An AI tutor that teaches teachers to use AI — by building, not watching
- 7-week personalized course: one conversation, one artifact, 20 minutes per week
- Every skill transfers to any AI tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude)
**Visual:** Skippy interface screenshot — clean, showing a mid-conversation moment. Or a simple diagram: Teacher → Skippy conversation → Artifact → Classroom.
**Speaker notes:** Keep this slide fast. The "how" comes next.

---

### Slide 5: How It Works — The 7-Week Journey
**Title:** "One win per week"
**Content:**

| Week | Focus | Teacher Builds |
|------|-------|---------------|
| 0 | Onboarding | Teaching profile (5 min) |
| 1 | Understanding AI | AI mental model card |
| 2 | Prompting | 4C prompt template |
| 3 | Lesson Planning | Lesson planning workflow |
| 4 | Feedback | Feedback/rubric template |
| 5 | Differentiation | Differentiation template |
| 6 | Ethics & Integration | Personal AI policy |

**Visual:** Timeline/journey map with artifact icons at each stop.
**Speaker notes:** Emphasize "artifact-first" — every session produces something the teacher uses Monday morning. Not theory. Not slides. A thing they made.

---

### Slide 6: The Learning Science
**Title:** "This isn't prompt tips. It's pedagogy."
**Content:**
- **SOLO Taxonomy:** Real-time diagnostic classifier assesses teacher understanding (5 levels: pre-structural → extended abstract)
- **Adaptive scaffolding:** Skippy's behavior visibly changes based on diagnosed level
- **4C Framework:** Context, Constraints, Command, Criteria — the transferable prompting structure
- **External testing loop:** "Try it in ChatGPT. Come back and tell me what happened."
- **One Win Then Wrap:** Every conversation follows a 6-phase arc toward one concrete output
**Visual:** SOLO taxonomy levels as a rising staircase, with Skippy's behavior noted at each level.
**Speaker notes:** This is where you win technical audiences and lose impatient ones. Know your room. For foundations: spend time here. For districts: go fast, hit "adapts to every teacher."

---

### Slide 7: The Personalization Engine
**Title:** "6 layers of personalization"
**Content:**
1. Global personality (warm British sensibility, expert tutor)
2. Week-specific pedagogy (learning goals, phase management)
3. Personalized opening (name, subject, grade level)
4. Teacher profile context (goals, constraints, time drains)
5. Conversation ledger (phase, SOLO level, 4C progress, artifact state)
6. Diagnostic probe (opening assessment each session)

"Every response Skippy gives is shaped by who you are, what you know, and where you are in the conversation."
**Visual:** Nested layers diagram (like a matryoshka or onion). Or a system diagram showing data flowing into the prompt.
**Speaker notes:** This slide proves depth. For tech audiences, this is the "wow, they actually built something" moment. For non-technical audiences, simplify: "Imagine a tutor who remembers everything about your teaching context and adjusts every response."

---

### Slide 8: Demo / Screenshots
**Title:** "Show, don't tell"
**Content:** 3-4 annotated screenshots:
1. Onboarding flow (personalized profile creation)
2. Mid-conversation with Skippy (showing adaptive response)
3. Artifact extraction (the thing they built)
4. Dashboard with progress and artifact gallery
**Visual:** Screenshots with callout annotations. Or a 60-second embedded video walkthrough.
**Speaker notes:** If presenting live, do a live demo here instead of screenshots. Have a pre-populated account ready.

---

### Slide 9: Traction
**Title:** "What's already working"
**Content:**
- **TimeSaveAI:** 100+ weekly active users on observation feedback tool — same design philosophy
- **AI Literacy PD:** Delivered at New Visions for Public Schools (Feb 2026) — one of NYC's largest education organizations
- **Product status:** Fully functional — 7-week course, Google SSO, artifact gallery, podcast recaps, personalized onboarding
- **Unit economics:** ~$5 per teacher for the full 7-week course
**Visual:** Metrics in large bold numbers. Timeline showing build velocity.
**Speaker notes:** Be honest about stage. "We're pre-launch with a working product. TimeSaveAI validates the approach. The next step is a formal pilot."

---

### Slide 10: Market
**Title:** "Timing + scale"
**Content:**
- 3.7M K-12 teachers in the U.S.
- $18B spent annually on teacher professional development
- DOL AI Literacy Framework (Feb 2026) creates compliance urgency
- AI-specific PD is <1% of market — category is being created now
- International expansion: 70M+ teachers globally
**Visual:** Market sizing funnel or concentric circles (US teachers → PD budget → AI PD gap → Our addressable market).
**Speaker notes:** Don't oversell TAM. Focus on the timing angle: "The federal government just told every school district they need AI literacy PD. We built it before they asked."

---

### Slide 11: Business Model
**Title:** "How it sustains"
**Content:**
- **Phase 1 — B2C Free:** Individual teachers, build user base and testimonials
- **Phase 2 — B2B Licensing:** $25-50 per teacher per year for district licenses
  - Includes: admin dashboard, cohort management, completion reporting, PD credit documentation
- **Phase 3 — Platform:** Advanced modules ($10-15 each), certification programs, curriculum licensing
- **Unit economics:** $5 cost / $50 revenue = 90% gross margin at scale
**Visual:** Revenue model diagram showing B2C → B2B → Platform evolution.
**Speaker notes:** The key insight: the expensive part (curriculum, classifier, architecture) is built. Marginal cost is API calls. Every new module is content, not code.

### Slide 13: Competitive Landscape
**Title:** "We're not replacing AI tools. We're the coach."
**Content:**

| | Skippy | Khanmigo | MagicSchool | ChatGPT Direct | Traditional PD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teaches teacher capability | Yes | No (student-facing) | No (generates for them) | No (no scaffolding) | Sometimes |
| Personalized to teacher | Yes (6 layers) | No | Partial | No | No |
| Structured progression | 7 weeks, SOLO taxonomy | No | No | No | 1 session |
| Produces reusable artifacts | Yes (7 artifacts) | No | Yes (but no skill transfer) | Ad hoc | Sometimes |
| Cost per teacher | ~$5 | $44/year | $99/year | Free-$20/mo | $50-200/session |
| Builds independence | Yes (external testing loop) | No | No | N/A | Varies |

**Visual:** Comparison matrix with checkmarks. Or a 2x2 quadrant (axes: personalization vs. capacity building).
**Speaker notes:** Don't trash competitors. Frame it as: "These tools serve different purposes. Khanmigo tutors students. MagicSchool generates content. We build teacher capability. They're complements, not substitutes."

---

### Slide 14: Roadmap
**Title:** "Where we're going"
**Content:**
- **Now:** Launch pilot, streaming responses, artifact export, error resilience
- **3 months:** Admin dashboard, PD credit certification, cohort management
- **6 months:** Advanced modules (special ed, ELL, STEM), voice mode, multilingual
- **12 months:** Curriculum licensing platform, district API integrations, efficacy research publication
**Visual:** Horizontal timeline with milestones. Color-coded by category (product, growth, research).
**Speaker notes:** The point: the architecture is built. What's left is polish, scale, and data.

---

### Slide 15: Impact Vision
**Title:** "The world we're building"
**Content:**
- Every teacher in America has access to personalized AI coaching — for $5
- Teacher AI literacy becomes measurable, not anecdotal
- The profession develops its relationship with AI on its own terms — capacity, not dependency
- "I think the question isn't whether you can use AI. It's whether you can use AI and still be recognizably yourself at the end of it."
**Visual:** Aspirational image — a teacher confidently using AI in their classroom, students engaged. Or a simple text slide with the closing quote.
**Speaker notes:** This is the emotional close. Slow down. Make eye contact. The quote is from Asher's own PD presentation.

---

### Slide 16: The Ask
**Title:** "What we need"
**Content:**
- **Funding:** $500K seed (or $150-250K grant)
- **Pilots:** 10 schools, 200 teachers, full data collection
- **Partnerships:** Districts ready to lead on AI literacy PD
- **Introductions:** Education foundations, EdTech accelerators, district CTO networks
**Visual:** Clean, four items. Contact info. QR code to the product.
**Speaker notes:** Be specific about what you're asking for. End with: "I'd love to show you the product. Can we schedule 15 minutes this week?"
