### About This Pilot

You are invited to participate in a pilot program for AI for Teachers, a professional development platform that uses an AI tutor named Skippy to help K-12 teachers learn practical, classroom-safe approaches to using AI.

This is an early-stage program. Your participation will help us improve the experience for future teacher users. Please read the following carefully.

---

### What You'll Do

- Complete a brief onboarding questionnaire about your teaching context
- Engage in weekly conversations with Skippy (an AI tutor) over a 6-week curriculum
- Build practical artifacts (prompt templates, lesson outlines, etc.) through guided conversation
- Optionally: listen to personalized podcast recaps of your sessions
- Optionally: provide feedback on your experience

**Time commitment:** Approximately 20–30 minutes per week for 6 weeks

---

### How Your Data Will Be Used

**During the pilot:**
- Your messages with Skippy will be processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate personalized responses
- Your professional profile (role, grades, subjects, goals) will be used to tailor your learning
- Skippy will assess your AI readiness level using an educational framework (SOLO taxonomy) to adjust conversation complexity
- Your conversations, artifacts, and progress will be stored securely in our database

**After the pilot:**
- Aggregated, de-identified data may be used to improve the platform (e.g., understanding which teaching contexts benefit most, where the curriculum needs strengthening)
- Individual data (your specific conversations and profile) will NOT be shared publicly or with your school/district
- You may request deletion of all your data at any time (see below)

**We will NOT:**
- Share your individual data with your employer, school, or district
- Use your data to evaluate your teaching performance
- Sell your data to third parties
- Use your data to train AI models

### Important Notes

**AI limitations:** Skippy is an AI tool, not a human expert. Its suggestions may be inaccurate, incomplete, or not appropriate for your specific school context. Always use your professional judgment.

**Student privacy:** Please do NOT share student names or identifiable student information in your conversations with Skippy. Refer to students in general terms (e.g., "a student in my 3rd period class" rather than using names).

**Feedback:** Your feedback is invaluable. We may ask you to complete brief surveys or participate in optional feedback sessions. This is entirely voluntary.

**No compensation:** This pilot is offered free of charge. There is no monetary compensation for participation.
