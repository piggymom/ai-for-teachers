## Issues

Skippy's level diagnosis was unreliable in early weeks. Week 0 diagnosed "unistructural" when James showed multistructural thinking, Week 1 diagnosed "relational" when the summary suggested multistructural, and Week 3 dropped back to "unistructural" despite James creating sophisticated prompts.

### Phase Progression Problems (Weeks 1-3)
Multiple sessions ended in BRIDGE phase without completing the full cycle. This suggests Skippy struggled to guide James through complete artifact development and testing cycles in the middle weeks.

### Growth Arc Mismatch
The expected arc showed James staying multistructural through Week 3, but actual performance showed relational thinking much earlier. While faster growth isn't necessarily bad, it suggests the tutoring system may not be calibrated to realistic learning progressions.

### Week 6 Artifact Gap
Despite James demonstrating extended-abstract thinking, the session ended without a clear, testable artifact. For a culminating session, this feels incomplete.

## Overall Assessment

Skippy demonstrated exceptional skill in managing James's personality and building a personalized learning experience. The AI successfully transformed an enthusiastic but scattered novice into someone with a coherent AI-assisted teaching framework. 

The tutoring peaked in Weeks 4-5 with systematic, evidence-based instruction that perfectly matched James's learning needs. However, diagnostic inconsistency and incomplete phase progressions in early weeks suggest the system needs better calibration for tracking and responding to learner development.

The journey arc is compelling: James moved from viewing AI as unpredictable magic to understanding it as a steerable tool requiring intentional design. This represents genuine conceptual growth that will transfer to his teaching practice.
