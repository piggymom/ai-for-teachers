City Budget Hearing: Fund Your Programs

You are the new Program Director at the Community Justice Center in Lowell. Tomorrow, you must speak to the city council about your budget request for next year. The city has limited money, and many departments want funding. You need to decide what to emphasize in your presentation to get the most money for your programs. Some council members care about crime numbers, while others want to hear about helping people change their lives. You only have time to present three main points about why your program matters. This choice will shape how the council sees your work and how much money they give you. Good decisions here depend on understanding recidivism. As a program director, you need to think about accountability before deciding. Your decision today affects which programs you can run all year.
