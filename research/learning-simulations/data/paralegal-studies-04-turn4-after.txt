You are working on a personal injury case for a client injured at a local Lowell warehouse. The other lawyer, Maria Chen from Chen & Associates, just sent your firm an angry email. She says your firm did not provide all the documents she requested during discovery. Your attorney, Mr. Rodriguez, is out of the office today. You must decide how to handle this situation. Chen is threatening to file a complaint with the court if your firm does not respond within 24 hours. This is a real professional problem that could hurt your firm's reputation in the Merrimack Valley legal community. The concept of Professional Courtesy plays a key role in this situation. How you respond now will affect whether this case stays on track or becomes a bigger conflict.
