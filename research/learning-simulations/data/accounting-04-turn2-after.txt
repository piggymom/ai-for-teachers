Payroll Error at Local Manufacturing Client

You discover a serious mistake in the payroll processing for Merrimack Valley Manufacturing, one of your firm's largest clients in Lowell. Last week, your junior accountant miscalculated overtime hours for 47 employees, overpaying some workers and underpaying others by hundreds of dollars. The error happened because the reconciliation process was skipped to save time during tax season. The client's owner found the problem this morning and called your office in a panic. Employees are already asking questions about their paychecks. You must decide how to fix this quickly while protecting both the company's finances and your firm's reputation. Good decisions here depend on understanding Gross Pay Calculation. This situation tests whether you prioritize speed or accuracy, and whether you follow proper procedures even under pressure.
