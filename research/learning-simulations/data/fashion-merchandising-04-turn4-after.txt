ROUND 1 OF 4
End of Season Overstock Crisis at Lowell Outlet

Your store in the Merrimack Valley Outlets has too much spring inventory left. Summer styles are coming in two weeks, but you still have 40 percent of spring clothes on the shelves. The problem is real: you ordered too much, and trends shifted faster than expected. You must decide now how to clear this old stock before the new summer merchandise arrives. If you do not move these items quickly, you will have no floor space for trending summer pieces. Your inventory health is already at risk, and holding old stock costs money every day. The Lowell community expects fresh fashion, and your competitors at the outlet mall are already marking down their spring items. Understanding Markdown is important for making the right choice here. You need a clearance strategy, which is your plan to sell leftover merchandise fast, but your choices will affect your brand image, sales numbers, and trend score in different ways.
