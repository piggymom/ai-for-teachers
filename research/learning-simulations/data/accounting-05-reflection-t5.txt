You just received a call from Valley Manufacturing, a local factory in Lowell that has been your client for three years. The owner thinks someone in their accounting department may have stolen money. She found expenses that do not match receipts. The company needs you to investigate the missing funds right away. This forensic accounting work could bring big fees to your firm, but it also takes a lot of time and special skills. Your team is already busy with tax returns for other Merrimack Valley clients. Good decisions here depend on understanding Audit Trail. You must decide how to help Valley Manufacturing while keeping your other work on track.
