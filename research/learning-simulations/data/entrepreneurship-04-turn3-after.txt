Your First Real Customer Wants to Meet

A local Lowell business owner reached out through your MCC networking event. She said your product could solve a real problem in her small manufacturing company. She wants to meet you this week to discuss a pilot project. But she has a condition: she wants a discount of 40% off your planned price, and she needs your product customized for her needs. This is your chance to gain your first real customer and prove your idea works. However, taking this deal will cost time, money, and team effort. How you handle Customer Acquisition will shape what happens next. You must decide if this opportunity is worth the resources you will spend, or if you should wait for customers who will pay full price.
