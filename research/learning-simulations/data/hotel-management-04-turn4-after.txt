UMass Lowell Conference Week: Price Now or Wait?

It is Monday morning, and your assistant just told you that UMass Lowell is hosting a major engineering conference next week. The hotel expects 85 percent occupancy without any price increases. Right now, your rooms are priced at 120 dollars per night. You know that conference guests will pay more, and your competitors are already raising their prices. However, you also know that many guests will book directly with you if they see fair prices on your website. Your ownership group is pushing you to maximize revenue per available room this week. You have three days to decide your pricing strategy before the conference guests start booking. Good decisions here depend on understanding Dynamic Pricing. The concept of RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room) plays a key role in this situation. This decision will affect your RevPAR, your guest satisfaction scores, and your reputation in the Merrimack Valley business community.
