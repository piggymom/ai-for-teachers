The Expert Witness Report Problem

You are preparing for a personal injury trial scheduled in Massachusetts Superior Court in Lowell next month. The case involves a car accident on the Lowell Connector where your client was injured. Your firm hired a medical expert to write a report about your client's injuries. Yesterday, the expert sent you the report, but there are serious problems with it. The expert's report does not include proper Legal Citation for the medical studies he references, making it hard for the judge to verify his sources. Also, the expert mentions he did similar work in New Hampshire courts, but no one confirmed whether his testimony is allowed under this court's Jurisdiction rules. You must decide quickly what to do because the filing deadline is in five days. If the report is incomplete or does not follow court rules, the judge might reject the expert's testimony, which could destroy your case.
