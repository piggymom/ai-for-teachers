Hotel Management

General Manager

LOWELL, MA — RIVERFRONT HOTEL NEAR DOWNTOWN

You're the new General Manager of a mid-size hotel on the Merrimack River in Lowell, serving business travelers, tourists drawn to Lowell National Historical Park, and conference guests from UMass Lowell and MCC. Balance the art of hospitality — creating unforgettable guest experiences — with the science of operations: dynamic pricing, staff retention, online reputation, and a demanding ownership group watching every KPI.

STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
💼 RevPAR
Revenue Per Available Room — the hospitality industry's most-watched financial KPI, calculated as occupancy rate multiplied by average daily rate (ADR).
⭐ Guest Satisfaction
The average rating guests leave on TripAdvisor, Google, and post-stay surveys.
👥 Staff Retention
The ability to keep your front desk, housekeeping, F&B, and engineering teams engaged and on payroll.
🏆 Brand Equity
Your hotel's reputation in the market — travel blogs, Condé Nast lists, Green Key certification, repeat guest rate, and trade press mentions.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Moments of Truth

Random high-stakes guest touchpoints surface each round — a VIP arrival, a complaint at 2am, a viral TikTok filmed in the lobby. If Staff Retention and Guest Satisfaction are high, your team handles it beautifully and earns five-star moments. If either is low, small issues explode into one-star spirals that ripple across OTAs for weeks.
