Lowell National Park Partnership Opportunity

The Lowell National Historical Park director called you today with an exciting idea. She wants your hotel to become the official partner hotel for park visitors. Right now, many tourists visit the park and sleep at chain hotels outside Lowell or drive an hour to other towns. Your hotel sits two blocks from the park entrance, making it the perfect choice. The park director offers to send you 30 to 50 park visitors each month through a special booking link on the park website. However, the partnership requires you to offer a lower room rate to park visitors, create a special welcome package about Lowell's history, and train your staff to answer questions about textile mills and the Merrimack River. You must decide quickly because the park director is meeting with a competitor hotel next week. This partnership could bring steady business, build your brand in the community, and attract guests who value cultural tourism. As a general manager, you need to think about Occupancy Rate before deciding. How you handle Yield Management will shape what happens next. But it also means less revenue per room, more staff training, and new operating costs that will stretch your team.
