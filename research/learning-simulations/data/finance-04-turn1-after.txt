The Inflation Problem: Protecting Client Savings

You work at Merrimack Valley Wealth Management in downtown Lowell. This morning, your manager shows you reports about inflation. Prices for food, gas, and housing are going up fast in Massachusetts. Your clients are worried. They ask: will my savings be worth less next year? You have several clients from Middlesex Community College and small business owners in the Merrimack Valley. They trusted you to protect their money. You need to decide now how to help them fight inflation. If you do nothing, their savings will lose buying power. If you move too fast, you might take on too much risk or break compliance rules. How you handle Inflation Hedge will shape what happens next. This decision will shape how your clients see you and how safe their portfolios really are.
