Local Business Wants to Partner with Your Program

A manufacturing company in Lowell called Merrimack Industrial Solutions wants to work with your education program. They need trained workers and are offering to donate equipment and hire your graduates. Right now, your program does not have strong connections with local businesses, and this is a real opportunity to change that. The company wants you to design new classes that match what their workers need to learn. This partnership could help your students find jobs after graduation and bring money into your program. However, you will need to spend time meeting with the company, changing your courses, and training teachers on new equipment. How you handle Workforce Development will shape what happens next. This situation is closely connected to Academic Partnership and how you manage it. Your faculty are already very busy, and some may worry that the company will control what you teach.
