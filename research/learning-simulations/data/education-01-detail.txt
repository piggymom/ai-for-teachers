STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
🎯 Student Success
How well students in your program are learning, progressing, and completing their credentials.
📈 Enrollment

The financial resources available to run and improve your academic program.
👩‍🏫 Faculty Morale
How motivated and satisfied your instructors and support staff are.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Accreditation Visit

Periodic accreditation reviews check your program's health. If Student Success and Faculty Morale are high, you pass easily and unlock bonus funding. If they're low, you face probation or loss of accreditation.
