UMass Lowell Conference Booking: Big Money, Big Risk

The UMass Lowell Business School wants to book your hotel for a three-day conference in eight weeks. They need 80 rooms, three meals per day, and meeting space. This is a 30,000 dollar opportunity that could boost your revenue significantly. However, you must decide now whether to block these dates or keep the rooms available for walk-in guests and smaller bookings that might come later. The conference organizers want a 20 percent discount because they are a nonprofit educational client. Your ownership team is watching your RevPAR closely, and this booking could lift your numbers or trap you with unsold rooms if the conference attendance drops. This situation is closely connected to Group Booking and how you manage it. This choice will teach you about yield management and how to balance guaranteed group revenue against the risk of losing higher-paying individual guests.
