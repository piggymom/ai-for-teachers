ROUND 1 OF 4
The Vegetable Trim Problem

You arrive at your restaurant near the Merrimack River in downtown Lowell on a busy Friday morning. Your prep cook is throwing away almost half of every vegetable that comes in. Yesterday alone, your kitchen discarded ten pounds of carrot peels, celery tops, and broccoli stems. You notice your budget is getting tight because you are buying too much food and wasting too much of it. Food waste is costing you real money each week. Your sous chef suggests you could use these scraps for stocks and sauces, but that would take more kitchen time. This situation is closely connected to Mise en place and how you manage it. Understanding Stock is important for making the right choice here. You need to make a decision now about how to handle the trim from today's lunch service.
