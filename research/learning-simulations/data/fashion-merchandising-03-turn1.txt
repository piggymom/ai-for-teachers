ROUND 1 OF 4
Fall Inventory Order: Spring Trend Data vs. Safe Classics

It is July, and you must place your fall inventory order for the Lowell outlet store. Your senior buyer has two different recommendations. The first recommendation shows that Gen Z customers in the Merrimack Valley are buying more oversized blazers and earth tones. However, your warehouse manager says the outlet store still has a lot of classic fitted jeans and black clothing from last season. Your sales team wants you to buy more trendy pieces to match what social media shows customers want. Your finance team wants you to use the old inventory first before buying expensive new stock. You must decide now because factories need your order by next week. If you order the wrong items, you will waste money on overstock or lose sales from stockouts. Understanding Seasonal Planning is important for making the right choice here. How you handle Markdown will shape what happens next. This decision will shape your entire fall season.
