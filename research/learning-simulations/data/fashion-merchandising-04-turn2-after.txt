TikTok Trend Takes Off — Do You Follow?

A popular TikTok creator with 2 million followers just posted a video wearing clothes from your competitor's Lowell outlet store. Within 24 hours, the video gets 5 million views and the store sells out of that entire style. Your store carries similar items, but they are buried in the back section with old inventory. You notice the trend spreading to Instagram and Twitter now. Your social media manager asks if you want to reposition your current stock to match this viral moment. This is a real opportunity to capture excited customers, but you must act fast. The trend could disappear in days, or it could grow into something bigger. How you handle Markdown will shape what happens next. Understanding Visual Merchandising is important for making the right choice here. Your choice will affect how well your store keeps up with what customers actually want to buy.
