Rising Health Insurance Costs Force a Hard Choice

Your company's health insurance costs just increased by 22 percent. The insurance company in Boston raised rates for all small businesses in Massachusetts. You must decide how to handle this expense before next quarter. Your 40 employees expect the same benefits they had last year. If you cut benefits or ask workers to pay more, employees may become unhappy and leave. But if you pay the full cost yourself, it will reduce your profit and limit money for other improvements. Understanding Benefits Package is important for making the right choice here. How you handle Cost-Benefit Analysis will shape what happens next. This is a real problem that many managers in the Merrimack Valley face right now.
