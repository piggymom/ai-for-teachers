Program Director

LOWELL, MA — COMMUNITY JUSTICE CENTER

You're the new director of a community justice program in Lowell, bridging law enforcement and community organizations. Balance public safety, community trust, rehabilitation, and tight budgets in a city working to reduce recidivism and build safer neighborhoods.

STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
🛡️ Public Safety
How safe residents feel and how effectively crime is being addressed in the community.
🤝 Community Trust
The level of confidence community members have in the justice system and your program.
🔄 Rehabilitation
How successfully people transition back into society after involvement with the justice system.
💰 Program Budget
The funding available to run your community justice programs and initiatives.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Cold Case File

Old cases and incidents from the community resurface unexpectedly. How you handle them reveals your priorities. Ignoring them erodes trust; addressing them costs resources but builds credibility.
