ROUND 1 OF 4
Health Inspector Finds Problem in Your Walk-In Cooler

Your restaurant is busy this Tuesday afternoon when a health inspector arrives for a routine check. She opens your walk-in cooler in the back and finds old containers of sauce pushed to the back shelf. The labels show these sauces are three weeks old, past the safe storage date. You know you have been saving money by using every bit of food before throwing it away. However, the health code requires you to throw away old food, even if it looks and smells fine. In Lowell's competitive restaurant scene, a health code violation can damage your reputation badly. Understanding FIFO (First In, First Out) is important for making the right choice here. You must decide now what to tell the inspector and how to fix this problem before she writes a report.
