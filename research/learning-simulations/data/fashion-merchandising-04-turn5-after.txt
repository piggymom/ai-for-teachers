ROUND 2 OF 4
Redesign the Store Layout Before Holiday Rush

Your Lowell outlet store is busy, but customers tell you they cannot find items easily. The current layout groups clothes by color, not by type or style. You notice that winter coats are hidden in the back corner, and trendy items are mixed with basic basics in the middle aisles. The holiday shopping season starts in four weeks, and your competitor two blocks away just opened a new store with a clear, organized layout that makes shopping faster. Your team is confused about which sections to restock, and sales have been flat for two weeks. A strong store layout helps customers find what they want quickly, which increases sales and improves their shopping experience. How you handle Visual Merchandising will shape what happens next. The concept of Floor Plan Layout plays a key role in this situation. You must decide now how to reorganize the store before the busiest shopping time of the year arrives.
