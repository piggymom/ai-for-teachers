Overdose Crisis in the Merrimack Valley

Three people died from opioid overdoses in Lowell this week. Local hospitals report that overdose emergency room visits have increased 40 percent in the last six months. Your community justice program must decide how to respond quickly. Community leaders are asking you to take action, but you have limited money and staff. Some people want more arrests and stricter enforcement. Others want to focus on treatment and harm reduction services. This choice will affect how safe the community feels, how much people trust your program, and whether people with addiction get help to recover. You must decide now because waiting will allow the crisis to grow worse.
