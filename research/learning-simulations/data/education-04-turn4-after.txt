Crisis in the Counseling Center

Three of your students came to your office this week. They told you they are struggling with stress, anxiety, and depression. They said they need mental health help, but MCC's counseling center can only see each student once every three weeks. The problem is real. Many students in Lowell work full time while studying. They have family responsibilities and money worries. These pressures hurt their mental health and make it hard to focus on school. Your school needs to decide how to help these students stay healthy and keep learning. Understanding Retention is important for making the right choice here. The concept of Holistic Support plays a key role in this situation. Without good mental health support, students will drop out, and enrollment will fall.
