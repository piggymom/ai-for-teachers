Head Chef & Owner

LOWELL, MA — DOWNTOWN RESTAURANT DISTRICT

You've just opened a restaurant in Lowell's vibrant downtown, steps from the Merrimack River. Manage your kitchen, delight customers, control costs, and build your reputation in a competitive food scene influenced by the city's rich multicultural heritage.

STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
⭐ Food Quality
The taste, presentation, and consistency of every dish that leaves your kitchen.
📱 Customer Reviews
What diners say about your restaurant on review sites and social media.
💰 Budget
Your restaurant's financial health and available cash flow.
🔥 Kitchen Morale
How your kitchen and front-of-house staff feel about working at your restaurant.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Critic's Table

A feared Lowell Sun food critic visits periodically. If Food Quality and Reviews are high, you earn a glowing write-up (bonus!). If they're low, expect a scathing review that tanks your reputation.
