Three Faculty Members Leave at Once

You just learned that three full-time instructors at MCC are leaving at the end of the semester. One is moving to a university in Boston. Another found a job in industry paying much more money. The third is retiring after 20 years of teaching. This means you have empty teaching positions right now, in the middle of the academic year. You need to fill these positions quickly, or students will not have classes to take. The problem is difficult because good teachers are hard to find, and you have limited money in your budget. Recruitment and retention are both critical issues for community colleges like MCC that serve working students in Lowell who depend on convenient class schedules. Your next decision will shape whether your program grows stronger or struggles to survive.
