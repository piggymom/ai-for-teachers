New Hire Diversity Program Proposal

Your HR manager brings you a proposal to start a diversity hiring initiative for your Lowell company. The plan would recruit more workers from the city's large Cambodian, Latino, and immigrant communities. Right now, your workforce does not match the diversity of Lowell itself. A local nonprofit called Merrimack Valley Community Partners wants to help you find qualified candidates from underrepresented groups. This initiative could improve your company's reputation in the community and bring fresh perspectives to your team. However, the program will require time, training resources, and changes to your hiring process. The concept of Stakeholder Engagement plays a key role in this situation. The decision you make now will shape what kind of employer your company becomes in Lowell.
