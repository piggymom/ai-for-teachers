Safety Incident at Your Lowell Warehouse

Last week, one of your warehouse workers in downtown Lowell slipped on a wet floor and broke his ankle. He was working in the shipping department when a water pipe leaked. The company paid for his hospital bill, but now you must decide what to do next. A safety audit shows your facility has many small hazards. You can spend money to fix these problems now, or wait and hope nothing else happens. Your workers are worried about safety, and the local Lowell community is starting to ask questions. As a general manager, you need to think about Hazard Assessment before deciding. How you handle Workplace Safety Program will shape what happens next. The choices you make will affect how much money you spend, how happy your employees feel, and what people in the Merrimack Valley think about your company.
