ROUND 1 OF 4
Many Part-Time Teachers Are Quitting

You discover that three adjunct instructors at MCC have given notice they are leaving at the end of this semester. These teachers taught evening and weekend classes for working students in Lowell. They worked part-time with low pay and no benefits, so they found full-time jobs with better pay elsewhere. Without these instructors, you cannot offer classes that hundreds of working students need. You must decide quickly how to keep classes running and prevent more teachers from leaving. This crisis shows how community colleges depend on adjunct faculty to serve students with flexible schedules. How you handle Course Offering will shape what happens next. Your decision will affect enrollment, student success, and whether your program can continue to serve Lowell's working families.
