Your Sister Needs $2,000 for Car Repair

Your sister calls you from work in Lowell. Her car broke down on the way to her shift at the hospital. The repair costs $2,000, and she does not have the money saved. She asks you to lend her the money. You just started your new job three months ago, and you have been working hard to build your emergency fund. This loan would take almost all of the money you saved. Your family has always helped each other, but you know you need this emergency fund for your own unexpected costs. The decision feels urgent because your sister cannot get to work without the car, and her paycheck is small. Good decisions here depend on understanding Personal Loan. This situation forces you to choose between helping family and protecting your own financial security.
