This is a real challenge to your business model and your ability to keep earning income. How you handle Robo-Advisor will shape what happens next. Good decisions here depend on understanding Competitive Advantage. Your decision will affect your portfolio performance, how much clients trust you, and whether regulators notice you are making risky moves to compete.
