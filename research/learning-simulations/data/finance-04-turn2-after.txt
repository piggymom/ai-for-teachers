Tech Stock Surge: Should You Move Fast?

You work at Merrimack Valley Wealth Management in Lowell. This morning, technology stocks jumped 8% after positive earnings reports. Several of your clients who work at local tech companies or who own tech stocks are calling. They want to know if they should buy more tech stocks right now while prices are rising. You must decide quickly because the stock market moves fast. One client, Mrs. Chen, who retired from a manufacturing job in nearby Lawrence, has asked you to help her understand if now is the time to invest more money in tech. Your decision will affect how much money her portfolio grows and how much risk she takes. If you push clients to buy now, you might gain quick profits, but you might also hurt them if prices fall suddenly. As a financial advisor, you need to think about Volatility before deciding. This is a real test of market timing, which is predicting when to buy and sell stocks for the best profits.
