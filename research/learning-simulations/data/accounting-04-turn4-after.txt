The Martinez Family Estate Plan Problem

Rosa Martinez is a local business owner in Lowell who runs a successful textile distribution company near the Merrimack Valley. She comes to your firm asking for help with her estate plan. She wants to make sure her three children will inherit the business and her house fairly when she passes away. However, Rosa has not organized her financial records in years, and she does not know the true value of her business or all her assets. You realize that a proper estate plan requires a detailed asset inventory and tax calculation first. Without this accurate information, the estate plan could create big problems for her family later. This situation is closely connected to Estate Tax and how you manage it. This is important work, but it will take many billable hours away from other clients during busy tax season.
