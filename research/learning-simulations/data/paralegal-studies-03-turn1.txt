ROUND 1 OF 4
New Client Intake Crisis at Merrimack Valley Law Offices

It is Monday morning at Merrimack Valley Law Offices in downtown Lowell. A new client walks in without an appointment. She is a woman from the Lowell community who needs help with an immigration case. Your senior attorney, Ms. Chen, is in court all day and will not return until 5 p.m. You have three other clients waiting for you to prepare documents, and your intake forms are incomplete from Friday. The immigration client needs to file paperwork within two weeks or she may lose her legal status. You must decide right now how to handle her intake interview and start gathering her case information. Your choice will affect how quickly her case moves forward and whether she feels the firm cares about her problem. Understanding Client Intake is important for making the right choice here. How you handle Conflict of Interest will shape what happens next. This situation tests your ability to manage competing demands while serving clients from Lowell's diverse community.
