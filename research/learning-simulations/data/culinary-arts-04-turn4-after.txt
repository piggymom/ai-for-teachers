ROUND 1 OF 4
Your Main Oven Is Breaking Down

Your restaurant's main oven stops working correctly during lunch service. The oven heats unevenly. Some dishes cook fast, but others stay raw or burn. You call a repair company in Lowell, and they say the oven is ten years old. They can fix it today for eight hundred dollars, but the part might fail again soon. A new oven costs four thousand dollars and will work perfectly for many years. Right now, you have limited money in your budget. You must decide quickly because your line cooks cannot bake bread or roasted dishes without a good oven. Your customers expect consistent food quality. Without a solution today, tonight's dinner service will be very difficult. Good decisions here depend on understanding Line Cook. Understanding Mise en Place is important for making the right choice here. This decision will affect your food quality, your budget, your kitchen staff's stress level, and your restaurant's reputation in the Lowell community.
