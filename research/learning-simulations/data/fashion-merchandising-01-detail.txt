Fashion Merchandising

Merchandising Director

LOWELL, MA — MERRIMACK VALLEY RETAIL & OUTLETS

You're the new merchandising director for a fashion retailer expanding into the Lowell outlet district. Source trends, manage inventory, build brand identity, and keep sales strong in a fast-moving industry where last season's hit is this season's markdown.

STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
🔮 Trend Score
How well your product selection aligns with current fashion trends and consumer demand.
🛍️ Sales
The revenue your store generates from merchandise sales across all channels.
✨ Brand Image
How consumers, influencers, and the fashion community perceive your retail brand.
📦 Inventory Health
The balance between having enough stock to meet demand without wasting money on excess.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Trend Forecast

Fashion trends shift each round — streetwear, minimalism, vintage, sustainable, or luxury. If your inventory matches the trend, sales soar. If you're out of sync, expect markdowns and losses.
