UMass Lowell Graduation Week: Price Now or Wait?

It is May, and graduation week at UMass Lowell is coming in three weeks. Your hotel sits two blocks from campus. Last year, the hotel was fully booked that week, but you charged standard rates and left money on the table. This year, your owner is asking why you did not raise prices during that peak period. Right now, rooms are priced at $129 per night. You know families will come to celebrate graduates, and hotel rooms will fill quickly. The question is how much to raise prices and when to do it. If you raise prices too high too soon, guests will book competitors or stay with relatives. If you wait too long or do not raise prices enough, you lose revenue that your ownership demands. This situation is closely connected to Dynamic Pricing and how you manage it. This is your first big test as the new General Manager, and the owner is watching your yield management strategy closely.
