Local Manufacturing Company Wants to Partner with MCC

A large manufacturing company in the Merrimack Valley called Precision Metal Works has contacted you about a partnership. They need trained workers for their growing production team and want to help design a new welding and engineering program. Right now, MCC does not have this program, but your college has the space and equipment to build one. This partnership could bring money, jobs, and new students to your program. However, the company wants students to start working part-time at their factory right away, while they are still in school. Your faculty are worried that students will be too tired to study if they work and go to school at the same time. The concept of Work-integrated learning plays a key role in this situation.
