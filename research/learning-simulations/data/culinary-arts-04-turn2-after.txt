ROUND 1 OF 4
Lowell Cultural Festival Booth Opportunity

The Lowell Cultural Festival is coming to downtown next month, and organizers want local restaurants to run food booths along the Merrimack River. This is a big opportunity to show your food to hundreds of new customers. You need to decide how to use mise en place and food cost control to make this work. The festival wants authentic dishes from different cultures that represent Lowell's diverse community. If you do this well, you will build your reputation and attract new regular customers. But preparing festival food takes extra time, costs money upfront, and pulls your kitchen staff away from your regular restaurant work. Your decision now will affect your budget, your team's energy, and your food quality for the rest of the month.
