You are organizing old case files at Merrimack Valley Law Offices in downtown Lowell when you discover a serious problem. This mistake would damage the firm's reputation in the tight-knit Lowell legal community and harm a vulnerable client who came to you for help. You have limited work hours left this week, and fixing this problem correctly will take significant time and focus.
