Personal Finance

MCC Graduate Managing Their Money

LOWELL, MA — YOUR FIRST ADULT FINANCIAL YEAR

You just graduated from Middlesex Community College and landed your first full-time job in Lowell. Between rent, student loans, a starter credit card, and the dream of one day owning a home, every paycheck becomes a balancing act. Build an emergency fund, protect your credit, start investing, and resist lifestyle inflation — all while real life keeps throwing curveballs.

STATS YOU'LL MANAGE
📊 Net Worth
Your total assets minus your total liabilities — the clearest snapshot of your financial health.
🛟 Emergency Fund
A dedicated high-yield savings buffer that absorbs life's unexpected shocks.
🎯 Credit Score
Your three-digit reputation with lenders, landlords, and even some employers.
😰 Financial Stress
The psychological weight of managing money — the 3am anxiety, the avoiding-the-mail feeling, the "I can't afford to think about this" freeze.
SPECIAL MECHANIC
Life Happens

Every few turns, real life strikes — a car repair, a medical bill, a surprise tax refund, a market downturn, a friend's wedding, a layoff rumor. If your Emergency Fund is strong, shocks become inconveniences. If it's empty, even small events spiral into debt and panic decisions.
