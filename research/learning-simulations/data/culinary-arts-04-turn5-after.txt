Your Secret Recipe Walks Out the Door

Last night, you discovered that Marco, one of your line cooks, took photos of your famous Merrimack River salmon dish while he worked his shift. This dish is your restaurant's most popular item and brings in steady customers to your downtown Lowell location. Marco quit this morning without notice, and you learned he is starting work at a competing restaurant two blocks away on Market Street. The competing restaurant is already advertising a new salmon special that sounds very similar to yours. You need to decide quickly how to protect your recipe and your reputation in Lowell's tight food community. Understanding Mise en place is important for making the right choice here. Understanding Recipe confidentiality is important for making the right choice here. Your staff is nervous about what happens next, and customers are already asking questions on social media.
