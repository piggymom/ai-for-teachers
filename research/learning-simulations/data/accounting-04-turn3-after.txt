Junior Staff Member Makes Tax Return Errors

Your newest junior accountant, Maria, just finished her first independent tax return for a local Lowell manufacturing company. You review her work and find five calculation errors and two missing documentation items. Maria is a good person and works hard, but she has not done this type of work before. She needs training to avoid these mistakes in the future. However, tax season is extremely busy right now, and you have many other clients waiting. You must decide how to handle Maria's training needs while keeping the firm's work moving forward. Training her now will improve accuracy and compliance for future work, but it will cost time and billable hours this week. As a senior accountant, you need to think about Internal Controls before deciding. The decision you make will affect whether your team stays strong and error-free or falls behind under pressure.
