Outreach Worker Shot in Merrimack Valley

Yesterday, Marcus Chen, one of your best outreach workers, was shot in the arm while trying to talk to gang members near Lowell High School. Marcus has worked for your program for two years and knows many young people in the neighborhoods. He was not killed, but he is now scared to go back to work. The shooting happened in broad daylight, and neighbors saw it but are afraid to talk to police. Your team is upset and worried about safety. This event forces you to decide how to respond to gang violence while keeping your staff and community safe. As a program director, you need to think about Gang Intervention before deciding. You must choose between aggressive enforcement, community healing, or focusing on jobs and hope for young people at risk.
