Your First Hire: The Salary vs. Equity Problem

You are building a web platform that helps small businesses in the Merrimack Valley find local workers. Right now it is just you working from the MCC Innovation Hub. Your first real customer wants to sign a contract next month, but you need a developer to build the features they need. You found Sarah, an excellent engineer who works at a local tech company in Lowell. Sarah is interested in your startup, but she wants $50,000 per year in salary. Your current funding runway is only enough to pay $25,000 per year or give her 10 percent company equity with no salary. This is your first major hiring decision, and you must choose carefully. The choice you make will shape your team culture and your relationship with investors. As a founder & ceo, you need to think about Equity compensation before deciding. How you handle Sarah's expectations will teach your first employee what working at your startup really means.
