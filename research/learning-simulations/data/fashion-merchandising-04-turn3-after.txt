Local Influencer Wants to Partner With Your Store

A popular Instagram influencer from the Lowell area has contacted you about a collaboration. She has 85,000 followers who are mostly women aged 18 to 35, and they follow fashion trends closely. She wants to create a limited collection with your store and promote it on her social media. This is a real opportunity to reach new customers and build your brand image in the Merrimack Valley. However, you need to decide quickly. The influencer wants an answer within one week, and you must choose how much control she has over the product selection and pricing. This decision will affect your trend score, sales, brand image, and how much inventory you need to order. The concept of Brand Collaboration plays a key role in this situation. As a merchandising director, you need to think about Inventory Commitment before deciding. Your choice today will show whether you can work with local talent to grow your Lowell store.
