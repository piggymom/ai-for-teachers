Police and Social Workers Clash Over Lowell Street Crisis

A homeless encampment has grown on Lowell Street near downtown, with about forty people living in tents. The Lowell Police Department wants to clear the area quickly for public safety. Your community justice program and the local homeless services organization want to move people to shelter beds and connect them with job training. Both groups asked you to choose a plan, but they disagree on how fast this should happen and who should lead the effort. The police say waiting causes more crime and disorder in the neighborhood. The social workers say rushing the clearance will push vulnerable people onto other streets without real help. This situation is closely connected to Interagency cooperation and how you manage it. Good decisions here depend on understanding Case management. You have one week to decide how these two agencies will work together, and your choice will affect how residents feel about public safety and how well people get support they need.
