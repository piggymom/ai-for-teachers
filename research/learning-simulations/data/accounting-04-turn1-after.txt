ROUND 1 OF 4
New Client Arrives With Messy Records

A manufacturing company in Lowell called MetalWorks Inc. just hired your firm to handle their accounting. The owner, Maria, says she kept financial records on paper and in three different spreadsheets for the past two years. She wants you to organize everything before tax season starts in eight weeks. Your firm needs to complete an onboarding process, which means reviewing all the old records, fixing errors, and setting up a clean system for the future. If you do this work carefully, MetalWorks will trust you and pay well. But if you rush through it or miss problems, you might miss fraud, make tax filing mistakes, or lose the client. Understanding Reconciliation is important for making the right choice here. This decision will shape how much profit your firm makes and how accurate your work becomes.
